About

Email marketing pricing is deliberately confusing. Every vendor prices on a different axis — subscribers, contacts, sends, "active" users — and buries the number you'll actually pay three sliders deep. This site exists to do the math out loud.

What we do

  • Price email tools at real list sizes (1,000 / 5,000 / 25,000 subscribers) instead of quoting the cheapest headline number.
  • Rank free plans by what they actually let you do, not by the word "free."
  • Flag billing traps: unsubscribed contacts that still bill, send caps on "unlimited" plans, free tiers that quietly shrank.
  • Re-check every pricing table quarterly. Each guide carries its check date; if the date is old, treat the numbers as stale.

What we don't do

  • Sell rankings. No vendor can pay for placement. Ever.
  • Pretend to hands-on test everything. We're upfront: our edge is pricing accuracy and currency, not lab testing. When we haven't used a tool ourselves, we say which claims come from documentation and user reports.
  • Recommend one "best" tool for everyone. A realtor, a course creator, and a restaurant need different things. Our guides pick by profile.

How the site makes money

Affiliate commissions. Some links to email tools earn us a percentage if you sign up — at no extra cost to you. That's the entire business model, and it's disclosed on every page that contains such links (full policy). It doesn't change rankings, and we'd rather lose a commission than recommend the wrong tool — a reader who trusts us is worth more than one signup.
