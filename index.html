How we work

  • Prices, not vibes. Every claim about cost is tied to a list size and a billing term, checked against pricing pages.
  • No sponsored rankings. The order of tools is never for sale. When a tool pays us a commission, you see the disclosure first.
  • Billing traps get flagged. Counting unsubscribed contacts, send caps on "unlimited" plans, free plans that aren't — we name them.
