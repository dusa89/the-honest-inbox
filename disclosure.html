Affiliate Disclosure

Effective: September 30, 2026

The short version Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and sign up for a paid plan, we may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you.

What this means in practice

  • We participate (or intend to participate) in the affiliate programs of the email marketing tools we cover, including but not limited to MailerLite, Kit, GetResponse, AWeber, and Brevo.
  • A commission is typically a percentage of your subscription, paid by the vendor — your price is identical whether you use our link or go direct.
  • Commissions are how this site funds itself. There is no other revenue: no sponsored placements, no paid reviews, no advertising.

What it doesn't change

  • Rankings are not for sale. No vendor can pay for a better position, a softer criticism, or removal of a billing trap we flagged.
  • We recommend tools we don't earn from when they're the right pick — and say so.
  • Pricing tables are built from public pricing pages, not from affiliate dashboards. A higher commission never buys a lower shown price.

FTC compliance

This disclosure exists to comply with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255): any material connection between us and a vendor — including affiliate commissions — must be clearly disclosed. You'll find a short disclosure notice at the top of every guide containing affiliate links, linking back to this page.

Questions

If anything about our business model is unclear, ask — contact details will be published here when the site goes live.
