Most email tools bill you for the same thing: contacts. Brevo doesn't. Brevo bills you for emails sent and lets you store unlimited contacts for free; MailerLite bills you for contacts and throws in generous sends (10× your subscriber count on Comfort). That single difference decides the winner — and the answer flips depending on how often you actually hit send.

TL;DR
Big list, rare sends → Brevo wins by a landslide (unlimited contacts, you only pay for what you send). Small list, frequent sends → MailerLite wins (automation from $12/mo, generous send allowance baked in). The deciding number is sends per subscriber per month — compute yours below, then match it to the table.

The billing traps

Brevo Starter has no automation. This is the biggest trap in the comparison. Starter looks half the price of Standard at every band ($9 vs $18, $32 vs $69) — but you're buying volume without workflows, sequences, or triggers. If you need automation at all, Standard is the real entry price, and it's roughly double the headline "from $9" price Brevo advertises.

Brevo's volume band must match your real workload. Unused emails don't roll over. A 5,000-email band ($18 Standard) for a program that actually sends 20,000 emails will force a mid-cycle upgrade to the 20k band ($69). Measure your sends for a month before committing — confirmations, resends, and welcome sequences all burn quota.

Shared-IP deliverability at scale. On Brevo's lower tiers you share sending IPs with thousands of other senders. Past ~100,000 emails/month, reviewers consistently flag that a dedicated IP becomes important — and Brevo's dedicated-IP world starts at the Professional tier ($499/mo). That's the real cliff in Brevo's ladder.

MailerLite's 10× send cap on Comfort. Weekly newsletters are fine (4 emails × your list = 4×). But add a welcome sequence, a re-engagement campaign, and a promo blast in one month and a heavy sender can hit 10×. Budget headroom of roughly 20% as a rule of thumb (estimate — verify against your actual send history), or step to Power.

MailerLite bills list growth, not sends. A growing list raises your bill every tier even if your send cadence never changes. Brevo's model is the opposite: list growth is free, send growth costs. If your list grows faster than your send volume, Brevo wins increasingly over time.

Pick Brevo if: you have a large list and send infrequently (monthly digests, event announcements); you want to grow contacts without growing your bill; you need transactional and marketing email in one account; or your list is 25k+ and growing.

Pick MailerLite if: you send weekly or more to a small-to-mid list; you want automation at the cheapest entry price in the category ($12–$19/mo); you value the simplest setup; or you'd rather pay by contact and never think about send quotas.

Migration notes

Brevo imports contacts free and fast (unlimited contacts means no dedupe anxiety), but automations don't migrate — budget an afternoon to rebuild workflows in Brevo's builder. MailerLite imports lists easily and its automations are simple enough to recreate quickly. Both count what they bill differently, so "dedupe your list" matters for MailerLite's bill and not at all for Brevo's.
