The 5 campaigns that pay

  1. The January document chase. Trigger: season open. Sequence: organizer link → missing-doc reminder ladder → portal-only delivery (no W-2s by email).
  2. The March reactivation. Trigger: last-year client with no contact. Send: “Your 2025 return is on file; new Schedule 1-A deductions could change your refund; book now.”
  3. The April extension save. Trigger: April 10 with no docs. Send: “Didn’t make the deadline? We’ll file your extension + finish by Oct 15.” Clarify: taxes are still due Apr 15.
  4. The estimated-payment lane. Trigger: quarterly calendar. Sends Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15 to self-employed filers — keeps firm top-of-mind and opens advisory upsell.
  5. The retention loop. Trigger: Oct extension finalize → Jan early-bird. “Filed on extension? You’re done. See you in January — early-bird pricing inside.”

The honest catches

Email is the reminder, not the vault. W-2s/SSNs should never move by email. Link to the portal. Email is the nudge; the portal is the safe.

Extension ≠ more time to pay. Taxes are still due April 15【2457692343325674281†L37-L38】. Teach it; angry clients kill referrals.

Free-file competition is real. 45% will only use free【7859273902670487330†L174-L176】. Your list sells security (96% feel secure with a pro【7859273902670487330†L21-L23】), not price.

Compliance is the moat. No refund guarantees, careful testimony, CAN-SPAM + financial lists. The tax firm that teaches wins.
