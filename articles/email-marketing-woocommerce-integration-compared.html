Why "WooCommerce integration" is doing a lot of heavy lifting

A WooCommerce store owner asks for a short list: sync the customers (respecting marketing consent), sync the orders and products in real time, fire abandoned-cart emails automatically, show which emails drove revenue, and run win-back flows on purchase history. Of the eight tools below, three do all of that through an official plugin, two hide the commerce features behind higher tiers, one has a platform-specific quirk that flips its verdict from the Shopify guide, and one has no official plugin at all. The rest of the article is receipts.

The bench, tool by tool

Brevo — the value winner (again)

Brevo ships an official WooCommerce plugin, documented in its own help center. It syncs customers, orders, and products in real time; adds an opt-in checkbox at checkout; installs the Brevo tracker to watch for abandoned carts and purchases; fires abandoned-cart reminders and post-purchase follow-ups; routes WooCommerce transactional emails through Brevo's SMTP for deliverability; and adds SMS campaigns and a live-chat widget on top. Carry-over from our Shopify guide: Brevo is the only tool in this bench with abandoned-cart and purchase automations on every plan, including free. On WooCommerce that makes it the cheapest complete cart-recovery setup on the list.

MailerLite — the platform flip

Here's the twist the Shopify guide couldn't give you. MailerLite's own help page on ecommerce triggers says WooCommerce supports all six triggers — Abandoned cart, Abandoned checkout, Buys any product, Buys specific product, Buys from category, and Purchase frequency. Shopify supports all except Abandoned cart. So on WooCommerce, MailerLite recovers abandoned carts AND abandoned checkouts, syncs customer and order data in real time, and can pull your products straight into landing pages. Two caveats from the same help pages: customers must be newsletter subscribers who accept marketing for the automations to fire, and MailerLite tells you to disable WooCommerce's own cart emails so customers don't get doubles. A budget pick with no quiet gap this time.

GetResponse — the deepest bench, with a gate

GetResponse's official WooCommerce integration page describes real-time sync of customers, products, and ecommerce data; adding customers to a list at checkout; abandoned-cart emails you can set up in a few clicks; and an AI product-recommendations add-on — all managed from the GetResponse dashboard, via its official WordPress plugin. It's the most complete commerce toolkit in the bench. The gate: abandoned-cart automation sits on a paid ecommerce tier. We won't quote a plan name or price here — the sources disagree, and pricing pages change — so check the current pricing page against your contact count before you commit.

Constant Contact — real native, no drama

Constant Contact's own knowledgebase guide for WooCommerce covers exactly the short list: automatically sync WooCommerce contacts into the account, promote products inside emails, and run automated abandoned-cart recovery. Nothing hidden, nothing missing — a straightforward native integration for stores that want the commerce basics to just work.

ActiveCampaign — the priciest path to commerce depth

ActiveCampaign ships an official "ActiveCampaign for WooCommerce" plugin, and the Deep Data integration is genuinely powerful — purchase behavior driving segments, abandoned-cart messages, conditional product content. But per ActiveCampaign's own help center, the WooCommerce Deep Data integration requires a Plus, Professional, or Enterprise plan, and historical sync is Plus-and-up only. On the starter tiers you're paying email-tool prices for an integration you can't fully use. If you're already on Plus, it's deep; if you're not, every other tool on this list gets you cart recovery cheaper.

Kit — creator-first, commerce-functional

Kit ships an official "Kit (formerly ConvertKit) for WooCommerce" plugin: it automatically sends customers and purchase data into the Kit account and can subscribe customers to a form, tag, or sequence at checkout. What the plugin's documented feature set does not cover is abandoned-cart tracking — this is the creator's tool, not the store's tool. If content and audience drive your WooCommerce store, it's a clean fit. If the cart is the business, look at Brevo or Omnisend.

AWeber — the thinnest

We could not find a brand-built AWeber WooCommerce plugin. The path to a connected WooCommerce store runs through third-party connector tools — the same class as the third-party app in our Shopify guide, with polling-style sync measured in minutes rather than real time. For a hobby store that's fine. For a store where email is a revenue channel, this is the weakest fit in the bench.

Omnisend and Klaviyo — the ones we'd actually pick for a store

The disclosure matters here: Omnisend and Klaviyo are not in our affiliate bench. We earn nothing if you choose them. And for a WooCommerce store where commerce is the business, they're the honest answer. Omnisend's official "Omnisend for WooCommerce" plugin — documented in its own help center — syncs contacts, products, and orders, automates abandoned-cart recovery and order confirmations, and runs segmentation with no API keys or manual field mapping. One honest catch, from Omnisend's own site: SMS opt-in at checkout is not supported on WooCommerce — you collect SMS consent through signup forms instead. Klaviyo plays the same commerce-native game. We include them because a comparison that hides the best answer for the category would be a lie wearing a disclosure.

The comparison at a glance

  • Brevo: official WooCommerce plugin; real-time contact/order/product sync; abandoned-cart + purchase automations on every plan incl. free; SMTP + SMS built in
  • MailerLite: official WooCommerce connection; ALL six ecommerce triggers incl. Abandoned cart (unlike Shopify); real-time sync; subscriber + marketing-consent requirement
  • GetResponse: official plugin; real-time sync; abandoned-cart emails in a few clicks; AI product recommendations add-on; cart automation on a paid ecommerce tier
  • Constant Contact: official knowledgebase guide; contact sync + product promotion + automated abandoned-cart emails
  • ActiveCampaign: official plugin; WooCommerce Deep Data on Plus/Pro/Enterprise only; historical sync gated the same way
  • Kit: official plugin; auto-sync customers + purchase data; checkout subscriptions to form/tag/sequence; no abandoned-cart tracking in the documented feature set
  • AWeber: no brand-built WooCommerce plugin found; third-party connectors only, polling-style sync
  • Omnisend/Klaviyo: commerce-native benchmarks — official Omnisend WooCommerce plugin with cart recovery, confirmations, segmentation; not in our affiliate bench

How to decide (the honest decision tree)

Store is the business, email is a revenue channel → Omnisend or Klaviyo. Commerce-native, no feature gates on the cart flows. We don't carry them; that's exactly why you can trust the recommendation.

Budget store that needs real cart recovery → Brevo. Free plan, official plugin, cart automation included — the cheapest complete setup in the bench.

Budget store, already in MailerLite's world → MailerLite. On WooCommerce (unlike Shopify) you lose nothing — abandoned cart works here.

Deepest toolkit, one vendor for everything → GetResponse, but verify the ecommerce tier on the current pricing page before you commit.

Content drives the store, audience first → Kit. Clean customer + purchase sync; don't ask it to recover carts.

Already paying for ActiveCampaign Plus or higher → use the Deep Data plugin and skip the rest. If you're not on Plus, don't upgrade just for this.

Do I still need Zapier? → For the core flows — customer sync, order sync, cart recovery — no: every tool above except AWeber covers those natively. Zapier earns its place for the long tail: niche CRMs, ad-platform audiences, spreadsheets, form tools outside the native bench. Our Zapier guide series covers the native-vs-Zapier line per tool.
